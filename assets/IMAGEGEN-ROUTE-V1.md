## Полный промпт

Create a premium editorial aerial photograph to serve as the background of a scientific water-sampling vessel website. Wide landscape composition, approximately 3:2. True top-down drone view of a calm freshwater reservoir: an irregular, gently curving natural shoreline with muted sage vegetation, a few pale grey rocks and sandy shallows occupies only the upper-left corner and the leftmost 18 percent of the frame. Most of the image (center, right, and lower two-thirds) is spacious, deep desaturated forest-teal water, with subtle natural variations in depth and very fine ripples. Soft overcast daylight, quiet precise atmosphere, realistic natural texture, understated premium photographic quality. Keep the center and right mostly uninterrupted dark water, suitable for a delicate diagram overlay that will be added separately. No sky, no horizon, no mountains seen from the side. No boats, no people, no docks, no buildings, no tropical beach, no bright turquoise, no text, no labels, no icons, no route lines, no borders, no watermark. Avoid dramatic waves or excessively detailed noisy water. The final image should feel like a real scientific field site seen vertically from above, not a fantasy landscape.
